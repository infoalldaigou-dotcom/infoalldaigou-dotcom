# AllDaigou Mall

## China buying assistance and parcel forwarding

[Visit AllDaigou Mall](https://mall.alldaigou.com/) · [Read the English user guide](https://mall.alldaigou.com/guide.html?lang=en)

Planning an order from China? Start with a clear request. AllDaigou Mall brings purchase and forwarding requests, quotations, inspection results and shipment updates into an order workflow. This page explains what to prepare before your first request.

### Choose the right request

| Your starting point | What to prepare |
| --- | --- |
| You need buying assistance for a product | Product link, exact size or colour, quantity and any questions for the seller |
| You have arranged the purchase and need parcel forwarding | Parcel details, tracking information and the receiving instructions for your order |
| You need to review goods before international shipping | The checks you want to request and the available inspection results or photos |

### First-order checklist for China shopping assistance

1. **Identify the item clearly.** Keep the product link together with the selected variant and quantity. Do not rely on a product image alone.
2. **Review the quotation.** Check product cost, domestic transport, service charges and international shipping separately. Confirm what is included before approving a payment.
3. **Keep parcel and order details connected.** Follow the receiving instructions shown for your request and retain the relevant tracking information.
4. **Review inspection information.** Check the available photos and results. Use order messages to clarify differences before the next step.
5. **Confirm the shipment details.** Check the destination, available shipping option and confirmed shipping fee on your own order page.

### How do I get started?

Open [mall.alldaigou.com](https://mall.alldaigou.com/) and consult the [step-by-step English guide](https://mall.alldaigou.com/guide.html?lang=en). The guide includes a captioned tutorial and an illustrated PDF manual covering registration, purchase or forwarding requests, quotations, inspection results, shipping and customer support.

The tutorial uses demonstration data. Services, available shipping options and charges depend on the goods, destination and confirmed quotation. Use your own order page for the current details.
